# Calcium supplementation for improving pregnancy and infant outcomes

**Source:** Cochrane Database Syst Rev. 2024 Nov 19;11(11):CD007079.
**DOI:** 10.1002/14651858.CD007079.pub4
**PMID:** 39560075

## Background
Maternal nutrition during pregnancy affects fetal growth and development. Women are recommended to increase calcium intake during pregnancy and lactation.

## Main Results
- **19 trials** with **16,625 women**
- Calcium supplementation probably slightly reduces preterm birth <37 weeks (RR 0.80, 95% CI 0.65 to 0.99; moderate-certainty evidence)
- Probably has little effect on preterm birth <34 weeks
- May have little or no effect on low birthweight (<2500 g) (low-certainty evidence)
