# Vitamin D supplementation for the treatment of COVID-19: a living systematic review

**Source:** Cochrane Database Syst Rev. 2021 May 24;5(5):CD015043.
**DOI:** 10.1002/14651858.CD015043
**PMID:** 34029377

## Main Results
- **3 RCTs** with **356 participants**
- All-cause mortality at hospital discharge: very uncertain whether vitamin D has an effect (very low-certainty evidence)
- Clinical status (need for invasive mechanical ventilation): may decrease need, but evidence uncertain (low-certainty evidence)
- Substantial clinical and methodological heterogeneity between studies

## Authors' Conclusions
Currently insufficient evidence to determine benefits and harms of vitamin D supplementation as treatment for COVID-19. Evidence is very uncertain. Urgent need for well-designed RCTs.
