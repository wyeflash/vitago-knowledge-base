NIH ODS — Probiotics Fact Sheet for Health Professionals

Definition: Live microorganisms that, when administered in adequate amounts, confer a health benefit on the host.

Evidence areas:
- Antibiotic-associated diarrhea: moderate evidence for prevention
- Clostridioides difficile infection: some evidence for prevention with certain strains
- Irritable bowel syndrome (IBS): mixed evidence, some strains may improve symptoms
- Inflammatory bowel disease (IBD): insufficient evidence for remission maintenance
- Lactose intolerance: some evidence for symptom improvement
- Respiratory infections: mixed evidence

Safety: Generally recognized as safe; caution in immunocompromised individuals, premature infants, and those with central venous catheters.
