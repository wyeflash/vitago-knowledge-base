Cochrane Review: Omega-3 fatty acids for the primary and secondary prevention of cardiovascular disease (Abdelhamid et al., 2018)

- 79 RCTs, 112,059 participants
- Moderate-to-high quality evidence: increasing EPA/DHA has little to no effect on mortality or cardiovascular health
- ALA may slightly reduce cardiovascular events but evidence is low quality

Cochrane Review: Vitamin E for cardiovascular disease prevention
- No evidence of benefit; possible increase in all-cause mortality at high doses

Cochrane Review: Folic acid for cardiovascular disease prevention
- No clear evidence of benefit in cardiovascular outcomes
