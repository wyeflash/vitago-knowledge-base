AREDS2 (Age-Related Eye Disease Study 2)

Sponsor: National Eye Institute (NEI/NIH)
Design: Multicenter, randomized, placebo-controlled trial
Participants: 4,203 participants aged 50–85 with intermediate AMD or late AMD in one eye

Key findings:
- Lutein + zeaxanthin (10 mg + 2 mg) did not reduce risk of progression to advanced AMD vs placebo, but showed potential benefit in exploratory analyses
- Omega-3 fatty acids (1 g DHA+EPA) did not reduce progression to advanced AMD
- The AREDS2 formulation (lutein/zeaxanthin + vitamin C + vitamin E + zinc + copper) is the evidence-based standard for intermediate AMD

Conclusion: For intermediate AMD, AREDS2 formula (with lutein/zeaxanthin, no beta-carotene) is the only supplement regimen with RCT evidence for slowing progression.
