# Insulin sensitivity and glucose regulation reference summary

Insulin sensitivity involves insulin receptor signaling, GLUT4 translocation, hepatic glucose output, skeletal muscle glucose uptake, adipose tissue inflammation and mitochondrial metabolism. Nutrition and supplement mechanisms are secondary to diet quality, energy balance, physical activity and medication management.

Key VitaGo extraction points:
- Magnesium, chromium, fiber, alpha-lipoic acid and inositol are commonly discussed in relation to glucose metabolism.
- ADA standards do not recommend routine supplement use for glycemic control without deficiency or specific indication.
- Low blood glucose risk and medication interactions are central safety concerns for users taking glucose-lowering drugs.
