WHO Noncommunicable Diseases (NCD) Framework

Key NCD categories:
1. Cardiovascular diseases (heart attacks, stroke)
2. Cancers
3. Chronic respiratory diseases
4. Diabetes / metabolic disorders
5. Mental health conditions
6. Musculoskeletal conditions

WHO recommends population-level interventions:
- Reduction of salt intake
- Elimination of trans fats
- Reduction of sugar-sweetened beverages
- Tobacco control
- Physical activity promotion

For individual-level nutritional support, WHO recognizes that micronutrient deficiencies contribute to NCD burden, but emphasizes that supplements should not replace healthy dietary patterns.
