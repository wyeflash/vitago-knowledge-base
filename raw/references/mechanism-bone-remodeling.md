# Bone remodeling reference summary

Bone remodeling reflects the balance between osteoblast-mediated formation and osteoclast-mediated resorption. Calcium, vitamin D, phosphorus, magnesium and vitamin K-dependent proteins contribute to mineralization and bone matrix regulation.

Key VitaGo extraction points:
- Vitamin D supports intestinal calcium absorption and calcium-phosphate homeostasis.
- Calcium intake matters most when dietary intake is insufficient; supplementation is not a universal bone-density solution.
- Vitamin K-dependent proteins are involved in bone matrix carboxylation; clinical fracture outcomes require separate evidence assessment.
