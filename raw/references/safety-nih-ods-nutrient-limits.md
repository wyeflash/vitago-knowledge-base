# NIH ODS nutrient recommendations and fact sheets — safety use

NIH Office of Dietary Supplements provides nutrient fact sheets and nutrient recommendation resources used by VitaGo KB to verify RDA/AI/UL, deficiency risk, tolerable upper intake levels, and nutrient-drug interaction warnings. For individual nutrient UL values, cross-check the relevant Health Professional fact sheet before publishing a dose table.

Key safety uses:
- RDA/AI/UL definitions and values.
- Nutrient-specific adverse effects from excessive intake.
- Medication interactions and special population cautions.
