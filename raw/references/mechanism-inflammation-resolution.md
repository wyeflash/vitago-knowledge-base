# Inflammation regulation and resolution reference summary

Inflammation regulation involves eicosanoid balance, NF-kB signaling, cytokine production, oxidative stress and resolution mediators. Omega-3 fatty acids EPA and DHA can be converted into specialized pro-resolving mediators, including resolvins and protectins.

Key VitaGo extraction points:
- EPA and DHA alter membrane fatty-acid composition and downstream lipid mediator profiles.
- Curcumin and ginger are commonly discussed botanicals with COX/LOX/NF-kB-related mechanisms, but clinical effects vary by indication and formulation.
- Anti-inflammatory mechanisms should not be translated directly into disease-treatment claims; disease pages must rely on clinical endpoints and guideline evidence.
