NCCIH — Milk Thistle

Traditional use: liver and gallbladder disorders
Active component: Silymarin (flavonolignan mixture), primarily silybin

Evidence:
- Hepatitis C: large NIH-funded trial (HALT-C) found silymarin did not significantly reduce liver disease symptoms or biomarkers compared to placebo
- Alcoholic liver disease: some small studies suggest possible benefit, but overall evidence is insufficient
- Amanita mushroom poisoning: intravenous silybin (legalin) is used in some countries as an antidote

Safety: Generally well tolerated; may cause gastrointestinal upset, headache, itching; allergic reactions in people allergic to ragweed, chrysanthemums, marigolds, daisies
