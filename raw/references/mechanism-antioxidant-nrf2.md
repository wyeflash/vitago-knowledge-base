# Antioxidant and Nrf2 response reference summary

The antioxidant defense network includes direct antioxidants, redox cycling nutrients and endogenous enzymes regulated partly by Nrf2/ARE signaling. Vitamins C and E, selenium-dependent glutathione peroxidases, zinc/copper-dependent SOD and compounds such as alpha-lipoic acid participate at different points.

Key VitaGo extraction points:
- Direct radical scavenging is only one part of antioxidant biology; enzyme induction, recycling and cellular redox signaling are equally important.
- More antioxidant intake is not automatically better. High-dose supplementation can be neutral, context-dependent or harmful in some settings.
- Nrf2 activation is a mechanism concept, not an efficacy guarantee.
