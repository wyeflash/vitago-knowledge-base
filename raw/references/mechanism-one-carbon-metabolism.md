# One-carbon metabolism reference summary

One-carbon metabolism integrates folate, vitamin B12, vitamin B6, riboflavin, choline/betaine and methionine-cycle pathways. It supports nucleotide synthesis, methylation reactions, homocysteine remethylation and SAMe-dependent methyl-group transfer.

Key VitaGo extraction points:
- Folate coenzymes transfer one-carbon units for DNA/RNA synthesis and methylation.
- Vitamin B12 is required for methionine synthase; deficiency can elevate homocysteine and produce neurologic abnormalities.
- Vitamin B6 participates in transsulfuration from homocysteine toward cysteine.
- The mechanism is relevant to pregnancy, anemia, neurologic function and cardiovascular risk markers, but supplementation is most actionable when intake is inadequate or deficiency/risk is present.
