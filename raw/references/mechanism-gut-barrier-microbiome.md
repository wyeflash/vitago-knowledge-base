# Gut barrier and microbiome reference summary

Gut barrier and microbiome mechanisms include epithelial tight junction integrity, mucus layer function, short-chain fatty acid production, immune tolerance, bile-acid metabolism and gut-brain signaling.

Key VitaGo extraction points:
- Probiotics are strain-specific and outcome-specific; results cannot be generalized across all products.
- Prebiotic fibers can increase fermentation products such as SCFAs, but tolerability varies, especially in IBS/FODMAP-sensitive users.
- Digestive and immune effects should be framed with strain, dose, population and endpoint boundaries.
