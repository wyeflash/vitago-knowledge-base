Cochrane Review (Deane et al., 2025 update): Omega-3 fatty acids for depression in adults

- 31 RCTs, various EPA/DHA formulations
- Low-certainty evidence that omega-3 may slightly reduce depressive symptoms
- EPA-predominant formulations (>60% EPA) showed potentially greater effect than DHA-predominant
- High heterogeneity across studies
- No clear dose-response relationship established

Safety: Generally well tolerated; common adverse effects include gastrointestinal symptoms and fishy aftertaste; bleeding risk at high doses with anticoagulants
