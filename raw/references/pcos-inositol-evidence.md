Systematic Review & Meta-analysis (Zeng et al., 2022): Inositol for PCOS

- Myo-inositol (MI) and D-chiro-inositol (DCI) improve insulin sensitivity in PCOS
- MI 2–4 g/day may restore ovulation and improve metabolic parameters
- 40:1 ratio (MI:DCI) thought to mimic physiological ovarian ratio
- Some evidence for improving fertility outcomes when combined with folic acid

Cochrane Review (Showell et al.): Inositol for subfertile women with PCOS
- Limited evidence; more RCTs needed before firm recommendations

ISSN Position Stand: Inositol shows promise for metabolic aspects of PCOS but evidence quality is moderate.
