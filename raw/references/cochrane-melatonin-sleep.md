Cochrane Review: Melatonin for the primary prevention of jet lag (Herxheimer & Petrie, 2002; updated)

- Melatonin is effective in preventing or reducing jet lag in adult travelers crossing five or more time zones
- Especially effective for eastward travel
- Doses: 0.5–5 mg; higher doses not necessarily more effective
- Take at destination bedtime

Cochrane Review: Melatonin for non-24-hour sleep-wake disorder in visually impaired people (Lockley et al. evidence)
- Low-dose melatonin (0.5 mg) can entrain circadian rhythms in blind individuals

NCCIH — Melatonin
- Moderate evidence for sleep-onset insomnia
- Mixed evidence for sleep maintenance
- Generally safe for short-term use
