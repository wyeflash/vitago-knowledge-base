# NCCIH herbs at a glance and supplement safety — safety use

NCCIH provides government-reviewed consumer and professional information on herbs, botanicals, and complementary health products. The content is used to verify safety warnings, pregnancy/lactation uncertainty, surgery and bleeding risk cautions, and herb-drug interaction concerns.

Key safety uses:
- Herbal products can interact with medications and may have limited safety data in pregnancy, lactation, and children.
- Traditional use is not equivalent to proven safety or efficacy.
- St. John's wort, garlic, ginkgo, turmeric/curcumin, green tea extracts, and sedative herbs require special caution depending on medications and clinical context.
