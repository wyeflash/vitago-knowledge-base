# USP / NSF / third-party certification — safety use

USP Verified, NSF certification, NSF Certified for Sport, ConsumerLab-style independent testing, and IFOS-like fish-oil testing are used as quality-risk reduction signals. They help verify identity, label accuracy, contaminants, manufacturing quality, or banned-substance screening depending on the program.

Key safety uses:
- Third-party certification reduces product-quality risk but does not prove clinical benefit.
- Certification scope differs by program; consumers must verify the exact mark and product listing.
- Athletes need sport-specific banned-substance screening, not only general supplement certification.
