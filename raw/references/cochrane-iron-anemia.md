Cochrane Review (Low et al., 2020): Iron supplementation for iron-deficiency anemia in pregnancy

- Daily iron supplementation improves hemoglobin and iron stores in pregnant women
- Intermittent iron (1–3 times/week) may be as effective as daily with fewer side effects
- Iron + folic acid more effective than iron alone for hemoglobin response

WHO Guideline: Weekly iron and folic acid supplementation as a public health strategy in populations where anemia is highly prevalent.

NIH ODS Iron Fact Sheet:
- Adult RDA: 8 mg/day (men), 18 mg/day (premenopausal women), 27 mg/day (pregnancy)
- UL: 45 mg/day
- Vitamin C enhances non-heme iron absorption; calcium, tea, coffee inhibit absorption
