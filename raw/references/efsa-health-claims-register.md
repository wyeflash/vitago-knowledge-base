EFSA Health Claims Register Summary

Article 13.1 claims (general function):
- Vitamins/minerals: normal function of immune system, normal energy-yielding metabolism, normal psychological function, maintenance of normal vision, normal blood clotting, etc.
- Fibre: increases faecal bulk, reduction of blood cholesterol
- Omega-3 fatty acids: maintenance of normal blood triglyceride levels (EPA+DHA), maintenance of normal blood pressure (DHA)

Article 14 claims (disease risk reduction):
- Plant sterols/stanols: blood cholesterol reduction
- Calcium/vitamin D: reduced risk of osteoporotic fractures
- Folate: maternal tissue growth during pregnancy, reduced risk of neural tube defects

Key rejected claims with insufficient evidence:
- Antioxidant vitamins and cancer risk reduction
- Most botanical/ herbal claims for disease prevention
- Glucosamine for joint health (mixed outcomes)

EFSA evaluation criteria: claims must be supported by human intervention studies with appropriate sample size, study duration, and validated biomarkers.
