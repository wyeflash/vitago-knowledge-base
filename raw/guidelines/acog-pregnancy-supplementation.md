# ACOG Committee Opinion No. 743: Folic Acid for the Prevention of Neural Tube Defects

## Summary

- All women of reproductive age should consume **400 mcg of folic acid daily** from fortified foods, supplements, or both, in addition to a diet containing folate-rich foods.
- Women with a **prior pregnancy affected by a neural tube defect (NTD)** should take **4 mg of folic acid daily** beginning at least 1 month before conception and through the first trimester, under medical supervision.
- Higher doses (4 mg) should not be used by women without such history due to potential masking of vitamin B12 deficiency.

## Additional ACOG Prenatal Nutrition Recommendations

| Nutrient | Recommendation | Source |
|----------|----------------|--------|
| Iron | 27 mg/day during pregnancy | ACOG / IOM |
| Calcium | 1,000 mg/day (ages 19–50) | ACOG / IOM |
| Vitamin D | 600 IU/day during pregnancy | ACOG / IOM |
| DHA/Omega-3 | 200–300 mg/day of DHA | ACOG |
| Iodine | 220 mcg/day (pregnancy); 290 mcg/day (lactation) | ACOG / IOM |
| Choline | 450 mg/day (pregnancy); 550 mg/day (lactation) | ACOG / IOM |

## Notes

ACOG recommends a prenatal vitamin for most pregnant individuals to help meet increased nutrient needs, especially folic acid, iron, iodine, and DHA.
