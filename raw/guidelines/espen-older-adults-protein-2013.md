# Older adults protein and nutrition recommendations summary

The PROT-AGE Study Group and geriatric nutrition guidelines emphasize that many older adults need higher protein intake than younger adults to support muscle maintenance, function and recovery, especially during illness or rehabilitation.

Key VitaGo extraction points:
- Healthy older adults often require attention to protein quality and distribution across meals.
- Vitamin D, calcium and vitamin B12 status are common nutrition concerns in older adults.
- Frailty, sarcopenia, poor appetite, dental problems, polypharmacy and kidney disease modify supplement decisions.
