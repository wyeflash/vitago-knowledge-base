# WHO NCD Action Plan — chronic disease guide context

WHO frames cardiovascular diseases, diabetes, chronic respiratory diseases, cancers and related metabolic risks as major noncommunicable disease priorities. VitaGo chronic guide pages use this source for the principle that diet, physical activity, tobacco/alcohol reduction, blood pressure/lipid/glucose management, and standard medical care are the foundation; supplements are adjuncts and must not replace disease treatment.
