# ISSN exercise and sports nutrition review summary

The International Society of Sports Nutrition review summarizes evidence and recommendations for sports nutrition, including protein intake, creatine, caffeine, hydration and recovery strategies.

Key VitaGo extraction points:
- Protein needs depend on training type, total energy intake and goals.
- Creatine monohydrate is one of the best-supported ergogenic aids for high-intensity performance and lean-mass gains.
- Athletes should prioritize food, training, sleep and third-party tested products before supplement stacking.
