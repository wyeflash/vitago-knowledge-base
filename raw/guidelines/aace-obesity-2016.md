# AACE obesity / overweight management guidance — supplement relevance

## Core principles

- Obesity is a chronic, relapsing disease requiring long-term, comprehensive care.
- First-line management is nutrition pattern quality, energy deficit when weight loss is indicated, physical activity, sleep, behavior support, and management of comorbidities.
- Initial clinically meaningful target is often 5–10% weight loss, but goals should be individualized.
- Supplements are adjuncts at most; they do not replace evidence-based obesity treatment, anti-obesity medications, bariatric/metabolic surgery when indicated, or care for diabetes, dyslipidemia, hypertension, MASLD, sleep apnea, or PCOS.

## Supplement-specific cautions

- Fiber and protein can support satiety and diet quality when integrated into a structured plan.
- Green tea / caffeine-containing products can increase insomnia, anxiety, palpitations, and blood pressure in susceptible people.
- Avoid products marketed as "fat-burning", "detox", rapid weight-loss, or stimulant blends with undisclosed doses.
- Weight-loss supplements have a higher risk of adulteration or unsafe pharmacologic ingredients than simple nutrient products.
