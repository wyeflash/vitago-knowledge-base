# Academy vegetarian diets position summary

The Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics position states that appropriately planned vegetarian and vegan diets can be nutritionally adequate, but certain nutrients require special attention.

Key VitaGo extraction points:
- Vitamin B12 is the most critical routine supplement consideration for vegan diets.
- Iron, zinc, iodine, calcium, vitamin D, omega-3 and protein quality require planning.
- Life stage and physiologic state affect risk: adolescents, pregnancy/lactation and older adults need tighter monitoring.
