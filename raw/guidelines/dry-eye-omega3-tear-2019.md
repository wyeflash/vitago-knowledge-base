# TFOS DEWS II (2017): Dry Eye Management and Therapy

## Summary

- Dry eye disease (DED) is a multifactorial disease of the ocular surface characterized by loss of homeostasis of the tear film.
- **Omega-3 fatty acids (EPA/DHA)**: DEWS II reported mixed evidence; some RCTs showed benefit while others (including the large DREAM study) showed no significant improvement over olive oil placebo. The report concluded that omega-3 supplementation may be beneficial for some patients, particularly those with low baseline omega-3 intake or meibomian gland dysfunction (MGD), but is not universally recommended.
- **Vitamin D**: Deficiency is associated with DED and MGD; supplementation may help if deficient.
- **Vitamin A**: Deficiency can cause xerophthalmia and goblet cell loss; not typically deficient in developed countries but important in malnutrition settings.
- **Omega-6 (GLA)**: Some small studies suggest benefit from gamma-linolenic acid (evening primrose oil, borage oil) for MGD.
- **Hyaluronic acid**: Topical use is standard; oral supplementation is emerging but not well-established.

## Key Nutrient/Supplement References

| Supplement | Evidence | Recommendation |
|------------|----------|----------------|
| Omega-3 (EPA/DHA) | Mixed | May benefit some patients; 1–2 g/day EPA+DHA; not universally recommended |
| Vitamin D | Low-Moderate | Screen and supplement if deficient; associated with DED severity |
| GLA (Omega-6) | Low | Evening primrose or borage oil may help MGD in some patients |
| Vitamin A | Strong (deficiency) | Supplement only if deficient |
| Lactoferrin | Emerging | Some RCTs positive for DED symptoms |
| Maqui berry extract | Emerging | Preliminary evidence for tear production |

## Notes

- The DREAM study (2018) was a large NIH-funded RCT that found no significant difference between omega-3 and placebo for dry eye symptoms, challenging earlier positive findings.
- Despite DREAM, many clinicians still consider a trial of omega-3 for patients with MGD or low dietary fish intake.
- Environmental modifications (humidifier, screen breaks, lid hygiene) are foundational.
