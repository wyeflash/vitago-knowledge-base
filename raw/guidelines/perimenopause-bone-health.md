# Perimenopause and bone health reference summary

Midlife and perimenopause are associated with changes in estrogen exposure, body composition, sleep, vasomotor symptoms and accelerated bone-loss risk around the menopause transition. Nutrition decisions should prioritize bone health, cardiometabolic risk and symptom safety boundaries.

Key VitaGo extraction points:
- Calcium and vitamin D adequacy, resistance training and fall-risk reduction are core bone-health measures.
- Some botanicals used for menopausal symptoms have mixed evidence and important medication or hormone-sensitive condition caveats.
- Perimenopause is a physiologic transition, not a disease; severe symptoms or abnormal bleeding require medical evaluation.
