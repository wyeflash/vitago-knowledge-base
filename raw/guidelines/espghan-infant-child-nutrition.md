# ESPGHAN Committee on Nutrition: Infant and Child Nutrition Guidelines

## Summary

### Breastfeeding
- Exclusive breastfeeding is recommended for approximately **6 months** (around 180 days).
- Continued breastfeeding alongside complementary foods up to **2 years or beyond** is recommended.

### Complementary Feeding
- Complementary foods should **not be introduced before 4 months** and **not after 6 months**.
- Introduction should be guided by developmental readiness, not just age.
- Iron-rich foods (meat, iron-fortified cereals) should be among the first complementary foods.

### Vitamin D Supplementation
- **400 IU/day** for all infants from birth to 12 months, regardless of feeding method.
- Continuation beyond 12 months may be needed depending on diet and sunlight exposure.

### Iron Supplementation
- **Term infants**: If complementary foods do not provide sufficient iron, supplementation of **1 mg/kg/day** (maximum 15 mg) from 4–6 months to 12 months.
- **Preterm infants**: **2–4 mg/kg/day** from birth to 12 months (may start at 2 weeks of age).
- Cow's milk should not be introduced before 12 months.

### Other Nutrients
- **Fluoride**: Not recommended in the first 6 months; from 6 months onward, only if local water is not fluoridated.
- **Vitamin K**: 1 mg intramuscular at birth (or oral regimen where IM is declined).
