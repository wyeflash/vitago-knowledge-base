Table of Contents Español Summary Magnesium is an essential mineral that serves as a cofactor for hundreds of enzymes
It is needed for a wide range of physiologic pathways, including energy production, nucleic acid and protein synthesis , ion transport, cell signaling , and has structural functions.&nbsp;&nbsp; (More information) Severe magnesium deficiency can disrupt vitamin D and calcium homeostasis
Individuals at higher risk include those with gastrointestinal or kidney disorders, those suffering from chronic alcoholism, and older adults
&nbsp; (More information) Inadequate dietary intakes and/or low serum concentrations of magnesium have been associated with increased risk of cardiovascular disease , osteoporosis , and metabolic disorders, including metabolic syndrome , hypertension , and type 2 diabetes mellitus
Magnesium supplementation may improve insulin sensitivity in individuals at risk for type 2 diabetes mellitus.&nbsp; Randomized controlled trials have also explored the use of intravenous magnesium to prevent complications after stroke or heart surgery.&nbsp;&nbsp; (More information) Intravenous magnesium sulfate is commonly used in obstetric care for the prevention of seizures in pregnant people with preeclampsia or eclampsia and to protect the brains of premature infants.&nbsp; ( More information ) Magnesium supplementation is currently being studied for its potential role in managing various conditions, including hypertension, cardiovascular disease, type 2 diabetes mellitus, asthma and pain.&nbsp;&nbsp; (More information ) National surveys indicate that over one-half of US adults have inadequate dietary magnesium intake
Good dietary sources of magnesium include green leafy vegetables, unrefined grains, legumes , beans, and nuts .&nbsp;&nbsp; (More information) Excessive magnesium intake from supplements can lead to adverse effects, especially in individuals with impaired kidney function
The Tolerable Upper Intake Level ( UL ) is 350 mg/day, and this applies specifically to supplemental magnesium
