# Adolescent nutrition reference summary

Adolescence is a period of rapid growth, pubertal development, bone-mass accrual and changing dietary behaviors. Nutrition priorities include adequate energy, protein, calcium, vitamin D, iron and avoidance of extreme dieting or unsafe supplement practices.

Key VitaGo extraction points:
- Iron deficiency risk increases in menstruating adolescents.
- Calcium and vitamin D are important during peak bone mass development.
- Sports, body-image pressure and restrictive diets can increase supplement misuse risk.
