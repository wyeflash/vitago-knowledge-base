# OARSI 2019 Guidelines for Non-Surgical Management of Osteoarthritis

## Summary

- Strong recommendation for exercise (aerobic, resistance, mind-body) and weight management for knee and hip OA.
- Topical NSAIDs strongly recommended for knee OA; oral NSAIDs conditionally recommended with caution for GI/renal/CV risks.
- **Glucosamine** and **chondroitin sulfate**: NOT conditionally recommended as core treatments due to inconsistent trial results (some subgroup analyses suggest potential benefit for knee OA with moderate-to-severe pain).
- **Omega-3 supplements**: No strong recommendation for OA specifically, but may be considered for comorbid CV risk.
- Intra-articular corticosteroids conditionally recommended for short-term pain relief.

## Key Nutrient/Supplement References

| Supplement | Evidence | OARSI Recommendation |
|------------|----------|---------------------|
| Glucosamine | Inconsistent | Not conditionally recommended (may try in moderate-severe knee OA subgroups) |
| Chondroitin | Inconsistent | Same as glucosamine |
| Omega-3 | Low for OA pain | Not specifically recommended for OA |
| Vitamin D | Insufficient for OA | Not recommended for OA pain unless deficient |
| Collagen (UC-II) | Emerging | Not in 2019 guideline; some newer RCTs positive |
| Turmeric/curcumin | Moderate (some RCTs) | Conditionally recommended in 2019 update for knee OA |

## Notes

- The 2019 OARSI guidelines are condition-based (knee, hip, hand/polyarticular) and comorbidity-aware.
- For patients with GI/CV/renal contraindications to NSAIDs, topical agents and non-pharmacologic approaches are prioritized.
