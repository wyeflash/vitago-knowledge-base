## Quality gates applied

- All substantive pages include external authority URLs in `## Sources`.
- All substantive pages include raw source pointers in frontmatter and `review_status: source_checked`.
- Redirect page is marked `review_status: exempt_redirect` and points to the substantive diabetes page.
- Safety wording avoids replacing standard treatment with supplements.
- Claims distinguish surrogate endpoints (LDL-C, TG, glucose, liver enzymes) from clinical outcomes.
